Bipkins™ Web Flasher & Setup

Flash companion firmware and configure Wi-Fi directly in Chrome or Edge via Web Serial.

Install Firmware

Step 1 of 2

Connect your ESP32 CYD board with a USB-C data cable and install the latest verified Bipkins™ release.

Web Serial Unavailable
Web Serial requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop over HTTPS. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.
HTTPS Required
Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or on localhost. Ensure you are accessing via https://.
Release: v1.0.0 Target: ESP32 (No PSRAM, 2MB Flash) App Offset: 0x10000
2

Connect to Wi-Fi

Step 2 of 2

The public firmware contains zero hardcoded credentials. After flashing, connect your Bipkin to your 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi to sync internet time and unlock pet care and hatching.

👉 Required Device Action:

On your CYD screen, tap the bottom banner: "Tap here: WiFi USB setup".
The screen will change to "USB setup ready (60s)". Then click Connect & Save Wi-Fi below.

WPA2/WPA3 Personal (8–63 characters). Credentials are stored directly in your device's private NVS.
💡
Ready. Tap "WiFi USB setup" on your CYD screen, enter your Wi-Fi details, and click Connect & Save Wi-Fi.
📋 Summary of First-Time Installation:
  1. Flash: Click Connect & Install above and choose your board's USB port (CH340/CP2102).
  2. Authorize: Once the egg appears, tap the bottom banner ("Tap here: WiFi USB setup") on the LCD.
  3. Provision: Enter your Wi-Fi network and click Connect & Save Wi-Fi.
  4. Play: Unplug and replug the USB cable. The Bipkin will connect to Wi-Fi, verify internet time, and begin incubating!
