Web Serial Unavailable
Web Serial requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop over HTTPS. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.
HTTPS Required
Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or on localhost. Ensure you are accessing via https://.

Internet & Activation

Step 2 of 2

Choose how you would like to connect your Bipkin. Both methods are 100% private and verified:

⚡
1-Click USB Sync
Instant • No Wi-Fi Needed

Leave your Bipkin plugged in. Click below to synchronize trusted internet time directly across the USB cable. Zero passwords needed anywhere.

📱
On-Device Touchscreen
Air-Gapped • Untethered Play

For playing on a wall adapter or battery without a computer:

  1. Tap the "SET UP WI-FI" card on your CYD screen.
  2. Select your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi from the scanned list.
  3. Type your password on the on-screen keyboard and tap Join.
  4. Your device activates and begins incubation completely air-gapped!
💡
Ready. Click Sync Internet Time & Activate above, or use the on-device touch keyboard on your CYD screen.
📋 Summary of First-Time Installation:
  1. Flash: Click Connect & Install above and choose your board's USB port (CH340/CP2102).
  2. Connect: Either click Sync Internet Time via USB, or tap the CYD screen to enter Wi-Fi directly on the device.
  3. Play: Once time is verified, egg incubation begins, distress care alerts activate, and hatching unlocks!
