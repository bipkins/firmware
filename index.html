Bipkins™ Web Flasher & Setup

Flash companion firmware and connect your ESP32 CYD companion in Chrome or Edge via Web Serial.

1

Install Firmware

Step 1 of 2

Connect your ESP32 CYD board with a USB-C data cable and install the latest Bipkins™ release.

Web Serial Unavailable
Web Serial requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop over HTTPS. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.
HTTPS Required
Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or on localhost. Ensure you are accessing via https://.
Target: ESP32 (No PSRAM, 2MB Custom Partition) App Offset: 0x10000
2

Internet & Activation

Step 2 of 2

Your Bipkin needs Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) to verify the internet time. Choose how to give it your network. The Wi-Fi name and password are entered on the device's own setup page or sent to the device over USB; this page does not upload them anywhere.

USB setup
From this computer
  1. Keep the Bipkin connected by USB.
  2. Tap SET UP WI-FI on the CYD screen, then USB Web Flasher Setup. This opens a 60-second window on the device.
  3. Open the USB setup page, select the USB device, enter your Wi-Fi name and password, and save within 60 seconds.
Open USB setup
Smartphone setup
From your phone
  1. Tap SET UP WI-FI on your CYD screen.
  2. Tap Phone Setup to show the on-screen QR code.
  3. Point your phone camera at the QR code to join Bipkin-Setup.
  4. A setup page opens on your phone. Select your Wi-Fi and tap Connect.
Tip: You can also type the network directly on the CYD with Touch Keyboard.
Summary of first-time installation
  1. Flash: Click Connect & Install above and choose your board's USB port (CH340/CP2102).
  2. Connect: Give the Bipkin your Wi-Fi, either with the USB setup page or from your phone using the QR code on the CYD screen.
  3. Play: Care and rewards stay locked until the Bipkin has Wi-Fi and has verified the internet time. After that your companion is ready to play.
