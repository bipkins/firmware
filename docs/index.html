Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or on localhost. Ensure you are accessing via https://.

Internet & Activation

Step 2 of 2

Choose how you would like to connect your Bipkin. Both methods are 100% private and verified:

⚡ Instant Activation via USB

Leave your Bipkin plugged into your computer. Your browser will immediately synchronize trusted UTC internet time directly across the USB cable—no Wi-Fi passwords required anywhere!

📱 Air-Gapped Setup on the CYD Touchscreen

For complete privacy when playing untethered on a wall charger or battery:

  1. On your CYD screen, tap the bottom banner: "Tap here: WiFi Setup".
  2. Your Bipkin scans 2.4 GHz airwaves and displays nearby networks. Tap your home Wi-Fi name.
  3. An on-screen virtual keyboard appears directly on the LCD. Type your Wi-Fi password with your finger and tap Join.
  4. Your device verifies the connection, syncs internet time, and begins incubation! Zero passwords ever enter your computer or browser.

📋 Summary of First-Time Installation:
  1. Flash: Click Connect & Install above and choose your board's USB port (CH340/CP2102).
  2. Connect: Either click Sync Internet Time via USB, or tap the CYD screen to enter Wi-Fi directly on the device.
  3. Play: Once time is verified, egg incubation begins, distress care alerts activate, and hatching unlocks!
