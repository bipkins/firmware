Web Serial Unavailable
Web Serial requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop over HTTPS. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.
HTTPS Required
Web Serial is only allowed over HTTPS or on localhost. Ensure you are accessing via https://.

Internet & Activation

Step 2 of 2

Choose how you would like to connect your Bipkin. Both methods are 100% private and verified:

⚡
1-Click USB Wi-Fi Setup
Fastest • Zero Typing on CYD

Type or paste your home Wi-Fi credentials below. Works with 63-character passwords, password manager autofill, and copy-paste.

📱
Smartphone QR Setup
Air-Gapped • Phone Hotspot

Configure Wi-Fi completely untethered using your smartphone camera:

  1. Tap "SET UP WI-FI" on your CYD screen.
  2. Tap "Phone Setup" to show the on-screen QR code.
  3. Point your phone camera at the QR code to join Bipkin-Setup.
  4. A portal opens on your phone. Select your Wi-Fi and tap Connect!
💡 Tip: You can also type directly on the CYD using the built-in Touch Keyboard with long-password scrolling.
💡
Ready. Enter your Wi-Fi details above and click Push Wi-Fi & Activate Bipkin, or scan the QR code on your CYD screen with your phone.
📋 Summary of First-Time Installation:
  1. Flash: Click Connect & Install above and choose your board's USB port (CH340/CP2102).
  2. Connect: Either push Wi-Fi via USB above, or scan the on-screen QR code using your smartphone.
  3. Play: Once connected and verified, egg incubation begins, care alerts unlock, and your companion comes alive!
